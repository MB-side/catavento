Flying at our sites

  1. Hold current SAFA membership. It is required to fly in Australia, including for visiting pilots.
  2. Contact the club before your first flight at any of our sites for site rules, protocols and hazards.
  3. Check each site's rating and supervision requirements. Some sites need a club safety officer or duty pilot present for lower-rated pilots.
  4. Join the club if you fly here regularly. Membership is open to current SAFA members, and members help administer the club's sites. [Fees and how to join, as published by the club]
