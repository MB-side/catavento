# 09 · Consultation Plan (for the founder)

**Status:** current · **Last updated:** 9 Oct 2026

## 1. Purpose

Use the Iteration 1 prototype to learn what pilots, clubs, schools and data owners value, what's wrong, and who will partner, before investing in Iteration 2.

## 2. Before sharing (checklist)

- [ ] Feedback form created and its URL set in `site.config.ts`; contact email set
- [ ] Anchor sites spot-checked against the Site Guide (§4)
- [ ] Every anchor site shows the right administering club
- [ ] Drafted New to flying and policy copy reviewed
- [ ] `/status` green for 7 nights
- [ ] Launch-week notes sent to data owners (§5)

## 3. Who and what to ask

| Audience | How | Ask |
|---|---|---|
| Pilots (aim ≥ 20, mixed PG/HG, novice to experienced) | Club newsletters and group chats (where rules allow), personal network | Would you open My Sites daily? What's missing on a site page? Is the club information right? What would make you come back weekly? |
| Clubs (aim ≥ 3 committees) | Email the president or secretary | Is your site and contact information right? Would you post status and notices here if it reached all pilots? What do you need for visitors? Would you let us show your station's data? |
| Schools and tandem operators (aim ≥ 3) | Phone or email | Is your listing right? Would enquiries from here be useful? Show `showPartnerPreviews` examples and ask what's acceptable |
| SAFA and a regional association | Email with a short summary | Does this help your goals (students reaching schools, events calendar, retention)? Any concerns? Data-sharing interest? |
| Data owners (Site Guide, Where To Fly, FreeFlightWx, ParaglidingEarth) | Launch-week note (§5) | Any objections or preferences? Interest in official feeds or collaboration? |
| New Zealand (NZHGPA, Auckland and Canterbury clubs, HighSide) | Short email with the NZ sample link | Is the sample right? Would NZ pilots and clubs use this? Interest in full coverage? |

**Feedback form questions:** who are you (pilot, club, school, other); what you used; what was useful; what was wrong (with page link); what's missing; would you use it weekly (yes, maybe, no); may we contact you (optional email).

## 4. Anchor sites for spot checks

Check name, coordinates, wind range, rating, administering club and station links against the Site Guide for: Long Reef NE, Long Reef SE, Stanwell Park, Manilla – Mt Borah, Mystic, Spring Hill, Lake George, Mt Emu, Killarney, Crackneck and Tunkallila (use the exact names the Site Guide uses). Single Hill was dropped on 2026-10-12: the Site Guide has no site of that name (docs/10 C30).

## 5. Launch-week notes ("notify, don't wait")

Short, friendly, specific. Adjust per recipient.

**To a data owner (Site Guide, Where To Fly, FreeFlightWx, ParaglidingEarth):**

> Hi {name}, I'm building Catavento, an independent prototype hub for Australian free-flight pilots ({url}). It links to {your site} on every relevant page and shows only {what we use, e.g. site names, coordinates and the short facts fields} with a clear credit and link back. Nothing else is copied. It refreshes {nightly/weekly} at a low request rate and identifies itself as CataventoBot. If you'd like anything changed or removed, reply and I'll act within 24 hours. I'd also love your feedback, and to talk about doing this more officially if you're open to it. Thanks for everything you've built for the community. {your name}

**To a club:**

> Hi {name}, I've built Catavento, a prototype hub that brings together Australian flying sites, clubs, schools and events ({url}). Your club's page is here: {club page}. Each of your sites shows your club as the administering club, with your contact details as published by {source}, and asks visiting pilots to contact you before their first flight. Could you check it's right? I'd also value 10 minutes of your thoughts on what would make this genuinely useful for your members and visitors. {your name}

**To a school or tandem operator:**

> Hi {name}, your {school/tandem business} is listed on Catavento, a prototype hub for Australian free flight ({listing url}), under our "New to flying" section for people looking for tandems and lessons. Could you check the details are right? I'm also keen to hear what would make enquiries from a site like this worthwhile for you. {your name}

## 6. Success signals to move to Iteration 2

- At least 2 clubs say they would post status and notices on the hub
- At least 3 schools or operators say enquiries would be worth paying for later
- At least half of pilot respondents say they'd use My Sites weekly
- No unresolved objection from a key data owner
